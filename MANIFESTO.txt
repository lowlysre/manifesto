THE LOWLY MANIFESTO

A software engineering manifesto

Version 0.2.0


    Rather be forgotten than remembered for giving in.

    Refused, "Summerholidays vs. Punkroutine"


We start from humanism: we judge software and the way it gets built by
what it does to people, not only by its profits. This is a statement of
what that requires of us.

Through this work, we have come to value:

  - People over profit
  - Understanding over speed
  - Maintaining over launching
  - Sustainability over heroics
  - Blameless fixes over finding fault


WE BELIEVE

  1. Software exists for people, and people pay for its failures.

  2. Not every user chooses to be one. Employers, schools, hospitals,
     and governments require people to use software, and those people
     can't leave. We owe them more for it.

  3. The people affected by our shortcuts can't see them, but they feel
     them.

  4. Profitable and right are not the same, and software should meet
     both bars.

  5. Someone has to operate everything we build, for longer than it took
     to build.

  6. Only a person can answer for what a model writes.

  7. People pay for our tools: low-paid data workers and authors whose
     work continues to be taken without consent.

  8. People learn by getting stuck. Take that away and they stop
     learning.

  9. Altruism is easy to exploit, and cheaper than fair compensation.

 10. A heroic save means a person absorbed a cost the system should have
     carried.


THUS WE RESOLVE THAT

  1. We ship only what we believe is safe for the people who use it.

  2. We refuse to build what harms people, and we say so out loud.

  3. We report risks early and in writing, even when it's unwelcome.

  4. We schedule, staff, and credit maintenance the way we do launches.

  5. We write for the next person: why we did it and what we didn't
     test.

  6. We own what an agent wrote, and we don't send work that costs more
     to review than it gives back.

  7. Nobody is forced to use AI, and nobody is shamed for using it or
     for declining to.

  8. We protect time to learn by doing, especially for people new to the
     work.

  9. When a mistake happens twice, we fix the system instead of blaming
     the person.

 10. Anyone can pause work for a concern without losing standing.


FINALLY

This is a living document. It will continue to be revised, adapted, and
improved. Feedback is welcome.
